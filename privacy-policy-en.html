Privacy Policy

Effective: March 17, 2026 | Last Updated: March 17, 2026

Animal Seeker (hereinafter "Service") values the privacy of its users and complies with applicable data protection laws. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

The Service can be used without creating an account. We collect only minimal information.

  • Automatically Collected: Device identifiers (advertising ID), app usage data, crash logs
  • Optional: Payment information when making in-app purchases (provided by App Store/Google Play)

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

  • Service operation and maintenance
  • Game progress data storage (locally on device)
  • App error analysis and service improvement
  • Advertising (when applicable)
  • In-app purchase processing

3. Data Retention

Game progress data is stored locally on the user's device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. Automatically collected analytics data is retained for a maximum of one year from the date of collection.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:

  • When the user has given prior consent
  • When required by law or legal process

5. Third-Party Services

The Service may use the following third-party services:

  • Google (AdMob): Advertising (when applicable)
  • Apple / Google: In-app purchase processing
  • Firebase / Analytics: App analytics and crash tracking (when applicable)

6. Your Rights

Users may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Withdraw consent for data collection
  • Request access to, correction, or deletion of personal data
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Limit ad tracking through device settings

7. Children's Privacy

The Service does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction). If a child uses the Service, parental consent may be required.

8. Data Security

  • Game data stored locally on device (minimal server transmission)
  • Encrypted communication (HTTPS)
  • Minimized access permissions

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in laws, policies, or services. Changes will be communicated through the app or this page.
